About Our Church

We welcome those seeking a place to grow in faith, learn the Bible, and worship the Lord in Spirit and in Truth. John 4:24

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel in light of the Three Angel's Messages, teach the Scriptures necessary in these end times, and encourage believers to live faithfully in Christ.

What to Expect

Friendly family fellowship, prayer, historical and traditional worship, and profound Bible preaching. Modest and welcoming environment.

Transistional Stage

As we work for the Lord and pray for a worship building where you can join us, we are committed to doing God's work online via YouTube and Facebook sermons, Topical Bible Studies, and Reactions to current events.
